Project overview

What I built and why it matters

I designed a web tool and Excel companion that organize a hypothetical client conversation into inputs, basic calculated indicators, product-category prompts, verification reminders, and follow-up steps. The structure supports consistency without pretending to replace licensed advice or bank procedures.

STAR summary
S

Situation

Early financial conversations can miss important context when goals, obligations, access needs, and verification steps are captured inconsistently.

T

Task

Create an educational tool that demonstrates disciplined discovery and documentation without manufacturing advisory experience.

A

Action

Built hypothetical inputs, conversation logic, calculated indicators, sample profiles, product-category prompts, and reminders to verify current rules and records.

R

Result

Delivered a repeatable web and Excel workflow that turns a synthetic profile into organized questions, review indicators, and next steps.
