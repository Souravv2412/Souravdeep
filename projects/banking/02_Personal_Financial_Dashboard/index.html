Project overview

What I built and why it matters

I created a six-sheet Excel model that connects sample transaction entries to budget-versus-actual reporting, monthly cash flow, savings-rate tracking, net-worth monitoring, and an executive-style dashboard. It demonstrates organized preparation for a financial or client-service review.

STAR summary
S

Situation

Transaction, budget, savings, and net-worth information can become fragmented and difficult to explain consistently.

T

Task

Build one organized Excel review that connects detailed entries to concise monthly indicators and follow-up discussion.

A

Action

Separated transactions, budget variance, net worth, monthly summaries, and instructions, then linked them with formulas and dashboard charts.

R

Result

The synthetic model calculates cash flow, savings rate, favourable budget variance, and ending net worth in one inspectable workbook.
